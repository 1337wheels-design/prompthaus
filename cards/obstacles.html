Quarter Pipe — Zone I · Bowl Session
FIELD
ZONE I
[ Field Spell — Üpark Zone I · Active While Card is Face-Up ]

Select 1 Bowl or Ramp obstacle. Until End Phase, you may attempt any grind or aerial on this obstacle. Coping attempts require a Speed Tribute. Land a 900° or higher: draw 1 Bonus Card. Tribute 1 Run to reset obstacle for next rider.

ATK: KINETIC
DEF: COPING LOCK

FIELD
ZONE II
[ Field Spell — Üpark Zone II · Active While Card is Face-Up ]

Select 1 Rail or Ledge adjacent to the stairs. Until End Phase, grind, slide, or gap freely. Handrail requires a Speed Tribute. Hubba Ledge is waxed — no tribute needed. Miss the landing: take 300 damage.

ATK: TECH
DEF: LOCKED GRIND

Long Fifty Ledge — Zone III · Precision Line
FIELD
ZONE III
[ Field Spell — Üpark Zone III · Active While Card is Face-Up ]

Select 1 Ledge or Manual Pad. Judged on Length + Control. Each rider: 30 seconds per attempt. Longest confirmed 50-50 wins the Cash Block. Wax pre-applied — no tribute. Bail within first 3 tiles: attempt does not count.

ATK: CONTROL
DEF: SLIDE LOCK

ZONE IV
[ Field Spell — Üpark Zone IV · Active While Card is Face-Up ]
