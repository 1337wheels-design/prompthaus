Royal Rumble — Brand Hosted Challenge Format

The Concept

Your brand nominates a Champ — a rider of your choice — to hold court at Üpark Payday 2026. A pool of attending riders steps up and skates against them in a live Game of Ü·P·A·R·K.

Your brand defines the reward structure. Your Champ represents your brand on the board. Your team is present to run the challenge and hand out drops as letters land.

The Champ stays the Champ. Champion status is granted — it cannot be won or lost through a single game. The pool plays to collect drops and to earn a title: the rider who deals the final letter to the Champ leaves the event as Skater of the Night.

Game Format — Ü·P·A·R·K

Letters are dealt to the Champ. The pool plays as a collective.

Pool Setup
Order is set before play begins — rock paper scissors or board throw elimination. Open entry. More riders in the pool widens the collective trick selection and raises survival odds.
Pool's Turn
The active pool member throws any trick any pool member can land. The Champ must match it. Failure deals a letter to the Champ.
Champ's Turn
The Champ sets a trick. Any one pool member landing it is enough to survive. The pool's size is its shield.
End State
Five letters — Ü·P·A·R·K — and the pool claims all drops. The Champ stays the Champ regardless. One activation per event.

Reward Structure — Your Brand Defines the Drops

One drop per letter dealt to the Champ. You set what each letter unlocks — sealed at registration, revealed as it lands.

Mystery drops are sealed at registration — physical or digital format, brand's choice. Drops are handed out by brand representatives as each letter lands, live in front of the crowd. Unclaimed drops (if the pool does not reach that letter) remain with the brand.

Brand Presence at the Event

  • Your team runs your Royal Rumble window — you own the zone for the duration of the challenge.
  • Your Champ is introduced by name and brand affiliation before play begins.
  • Brand can set the scene: music, presentation, signage within the challenge zone.
  • Brand representatives hand drops directly to the pool as letters land — no intermediary.
  • Mystery drop reveal is your moment — format, timing, and theatre are yours to design.
  • One Royal Rumble slot per brand per event. Slot timing confirmed at registration.
